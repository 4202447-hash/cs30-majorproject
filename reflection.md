## Hardest part
There were many challenges in making this project. The two hardest ones were making the mobs, and managing the performance. Making each individual mob took me a long time a they all had unique ways of movement, and had unique attacks/attack patterns. Making mobs turn around, and detect empty slots in the grid was also a challenge. In terms of performance the game experienced extreme lag as the 2D grid of the map is 200 x 150.
